how to join us

  1. Make sure your website includes a way to contact you. If you write a blog, please provide an RSS feed so others can follow your posts.
  2. Either email us your name, desired ID, and website URL, or open a pull request adding yourself to members.json. We will review your submission and add you as soon as possible.
  3. Once accepted, add the webring links to your website: 
    <div>
    <a href="https://webring-vit.vercel.app">backdoor</a>
    <a href="https://webring-vit.vercel.app/redirect?from=your-id&dir=prev">&larr; prev</a>
    <a href="https://webring-vit.vercel.app/random">&#9851; random</a>
    <a href="https://webring-vit.vercel.app/redirect?from=your-id&dir=next">next &rarr;</a>
</div>
  4. See you around the ring. Don't forget to explore your neighbours!
